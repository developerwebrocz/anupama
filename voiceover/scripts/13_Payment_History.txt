For example, first installment లో fifty thousand rupees advance payment record చేసి, corresponding invoice generate చేయండి.
Later twenty five thousand rupees payment receive అయితే, Update Payment button మీద click చేసి, Add Payment లో amount and payment mode select చేసి, Submit button మీద click చేయండి.
అలాగే final payment twenty five thousand, plus eighteen percent GST, total forty three thousand enter చేసి update చేయొచ్చు.
ఇలా different dates and stages లో receive అయ్యే payments ని manage చేయొచ్చు.
Side menu లో ఉన్న Invoice History లో total received amount and pending balance కూడా one place లో check చేయొచ్చు.
